# Bluff interface direction

- Keep the business interface simple: Dashboard, Events, Contracts.
- No eyebrow text or uppercase marketing labels above headings.
- No explanatory subcaptions under page headings, metric cards, section headings or flow steps.
- Show actual data (dates, venues, triggers, prices, status) where it is needed. Errors, transaction-mode labels and save feedback remain visible.
- Use Geist with 400 and 600 weights. Console sizes: 12px secondary data, 14px body, 20px page headings, 30px metric values.
- Neutral gray surfaces, restrained borders, small radii, and compact spacing. Use color for selected protection and meaningful status.
- Use Untitled UI interface icons through the existing icon component.
- Keep Gemini orders, Arc transactions and QuickNode evidence inside contract details or Integrations.
- Treat the customer checkout as one step in the business demo. Do not add a storefront browsing layer.
