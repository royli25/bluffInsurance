# Bluff interface direction

- Keep the business interface simple: Dashboard, Events, Contracts.
- No eyebrow text or uppercase marketing labels above headings.
- No explanatory subcaptions under page headings, metric cards, section headings or flow steps.
- Show actual data (dates, venues, triggers, prices, status) where it is needed. Errors, transaction-mode labels and save feedback remain visible.
- Use Geist with 400 and 600 weights. Console sizes: 12px secondary data, 14px body, 20px page headings, 30px metric values.
- Keep the business interface on dark neutral gray surfaces, restrained borders, small radii, and compact spacing. Use color for selected protection and meaningful status.
- Use Untitled UI interface icons through the existing icon component.
- Keep Gemini orders, Arc transactions and QuickNode evidence inside contract details or Integrations.
- The customer interface is independent: event selection → ticket and protection selection → order confirmation. It has no business navigation or settlement controls.
- Customer screens use Ticketmaster-style blue (#024ddf), white cards, a light gray canvas, and blue selection states. Keep the simple full-width flow.
- Business and customer interfaces share market, offer, and order data, but have separate entry points and launch modes.

- Separate panels with surface shades and spacing. Avoid decorative panel strokes and nested outlines; retain focus outlines and necessary input affordances.
- Use sourced provider wordmarks with preserved proportions. Gemini belongs with pricing; Arc and Quicknode belong with settlement and integration evidence.
- Use product language throughout the visible interface. No demo, simulation, hackathon, or placeholder-payment copy. Technical evidence must still accurately say when a transaction has not been submitted.
